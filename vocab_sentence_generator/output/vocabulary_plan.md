### Sentences

1. I **have** **one** **new** plan, and **two** **other** ideas, but **this** **time** **is** **not** **up** yet: **there** **aren't** **the** **people** **to** blame **for** **it**; **yes**, **that** **year** **we** **didn't** **do** **so** **in** **the** **city** **of** **time**, **and** **that** **is** **so** **that** **he** **be** **not** **there** **to** **it**.

### Sentences

1. First, **you** should **say** what **way** you will **go**; **or** you can stay **on** the **high** hill **now** and watch the **man**. **As** **he** speaks, **her** friend asks, “**What** is **their** plan **for** the **day**?” **Just** then, **they** show **three** notes, **which** explain **all** the steps, and **my** notebook says, “If you want to **go out**, do it **first**.”

中文翻译：首先，你应该说清楚你要走哪条路；否则你可以现在待在高高的山坡上看那个男人。随着他开口说话，她的朋友问：“你们的计划是为了这一天做什么？”就在这时，他们拿出三张便条，上面解释了所有步骤，而我的笔记写着：“如果你想出去，先从第一步开始。”

2. Last week, **he** told **me** that **good** ideas can come **with** patience, **and** **if** **you** listen carefully, you will find the **out** answer. **Now** **three** **old** books sit **on** the table; **which** one is **for** **you**? **All** I can say is that **her** story and **their** story are different, **but** they lead to the same **way**.

中文翻译：上周，他告诉我，好主意要靠耐心；而如果你仔细听，你就会找到答案。现在，三本旧书放在桌上；哪一本是给你的？我能说的只有：她的故事和他们的故事不一样，但它们通向同一条路。

### Sentences

1. When **we** meet **your** **great** **woman** guide at **four** in the lobby, she will **get** **some** **thing** done **then**; **how** **big** **life** can be depends on **what** **we** choose, **because** the next step **would** **make** **our** future **more** hopeful than the past.

中文翻译：当我们在大厅于四点遇到你那位很棒的女导游时，她会在然后把一些事情办好；生活有多大取决于我们选择什么，因为下一步会让我们的未来比过去更有希望。

2. I asked **how** **she** learned **these** skills **from** an **American** trainer, and she said, “**Then** I practiced **by** myself, and **more** and **more** people noticed **its** results; **next** year, **we** will help **some** students, and **four** million will benefit, **than** before.”

中文翻译：我问她这些技能是怎么从一位美国教练那里学到的，她说：“然后我自己练习，越来越多的人注意到了它的效果；明年，我们会帮助一些学生，四百万人会受益，比以前更多。”

3. If **you** want to know **when** to **get** the best answer, ask **who** designed the plan, **because** **our** success is **more** than luck, and **more** effort will come **from** the right habit at **the** right time.

中文翻译：如果你想知道什么时候才能得到最好的答案，就去问是谁设计了这个计划，因为我们的成功不只是运气，而是来自正确习惯在对的时间里的更多努力。
